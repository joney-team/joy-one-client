## Core Modules

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Real-time overview of your business metrics |
| **Tasks** | Kanban board, Gantt chart, and time tracking |
| **Customers** | CRM with contacts, KYC, and customer history |
| **Scheduling** | Appointment and booking management |
| **Products** | Catalog, categories, combos, and stock |
| **Orders** | Sales and order lifecycle management |
| **Invoices** | Electronic invoice generation and tracking |
| **Promotions** | Discount campaigns and voucher management |
| **Messages** | Unified inbox — Zalo OA & Messenger |

## Getting Started

1. Log in to your workspace at [joyone.com](https://joyone.com)
2. Set up your workspace profile and invite team members
3. Configure your products, customers, and services
4. Start managing orders, schedules, and communications in one place
