# Customers

The Customers module is JoyOne's built-in CRM. Store and manage all information about your clients in one place — contacts, history, KYC documents, and more.

## Customer Profile

Each customer record contains:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Full name or business name |
| **Phone / Email** | Primary contact details |
| **Tags** | Labels for segmentation (e.g. VIP, Wholesale) |
| **Assigned staff** | Who is responsible for this customer |
| **Custom fields** | Flexible fields defined per workspace |
| **Notes** | Internal freeform notes |

## Activity Timeline

Every interaction is logged automatically:
- Orders placed
- Appointments booked
- Messages sent/received
- Tasks linked to this customer
- Invoice history

## Contacts

A customer can have **multiple contacts** (e.g. different decision-makers in a company). Each contact has its own name, phone, email, and role.

## KYC (Know Your Customer)

<Callout type="info">
  KYC is primarily used in financial or compliance-sensitive businesses. Enable it per workspace in **Settings → Customer Forms**.
</Callout>

Upload and verify identity documents:
- ID card / Passport
- Business registration certificate
- Tax code
- Other custom documents

KYC status: **Pending → Verified → Rejected**

## Customer Forms

Create custom intake forms to collect data from customers. Forms can be shared via link or embedded on your website. Responses are automatically attached to the customer record.

## Adding a Customer

<Steps>
  ### Go to Customers
  Click **Customers** in the left navigation.

  ### Create new
  Click **+ New Customer** (top right).

  ### Fill basic info
  Enter name, phone, email, and any custom fields.

  ### Save
  Click **Save**. The customer is now searchable and can be linked to orders, tasks, and bookings.
</Steps>

## Import / Export

- **Import** customers from CSV — download the template, fill it in, and upload.
- **Export** the full customer list or a filtered subset to CSV at any time.

## Segmentation & Search

Use the search bar and filter panel to find customers by:
- Name, phone, or email
- Tags / Labels
- Assigned staff
- Last activity date
- KYC status
